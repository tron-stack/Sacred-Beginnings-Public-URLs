Getting Started

Everything you need to know to begin your Sacred Beginnings journey.

How to Create an Account

  1. Download Sacred Beginnings from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android).
  2. Tap "Create Account" on the welcome screen.
  3. Enter your name, email address, and choose a secure password.
  4. Check your inbox for a verification email and tap the confirmation link.
  5. Complete the onboarding flow: select your journey type, cycle history, spiritual preferences, and partner status.
  6. You're ready to go! Your home screen will be personalized based on your selections.

Setting Up Cycle Tracking

During onboarding (or anytime from the Cycle tab), you can set up your cycle tracking:

  • Log your last period: Enter the start date of your most recent period. This helps the app calculate your first predictions.
  • Average cycle length: If you know your typical cycle length (e.g., 28 days), enter it for more accurate initial predictions. Don't worry if you're unsure — the app learns from your data over time.
  • Daily logging: Each day, log any relevant data — period flow, basal body temperature (BBT), cervical mucus, OPK results, symptoms, and mood. The more data you provide, the smarter your predictions become.
  • Calendar view: Your calendar will color-code period days, fertile window, predicted ovulation, and next period as data accumulates.

Connecting Apple Health / Health Connect

  1. Open Sacred Beginnings and go to Profile > Settings > Health Connection.
  2. Tap "Connect Apple Health" (iOS) or "Connect Health Connect" (Android).
  3. Review the requested permissions (sleep, steps, heart rate, HRV, BBT, water, weight, menstruation) and approve the ones you'd like to share.
  4. Your health data will automatically sync to the app and appear on your Wellness Dashboard.
  5. You can change permissions at any time in your device's system settings.

Your health data stays private. We never sell, share, or use your health data for advertising. You can view exactly what data we access in Profile > Health Data Transparency.

Navigating the App

The main navigation tabs at the bottom of the screen give you quick access to the core features:

  • Home: Your personalized daily dashboard with alignment score, quick actions, today's insights, and upcoming reminders.
  • Cycle / Pregnancy / PostPartum: This tab adapts to your life stage — cycle tracking with calendar and daily logging, pregnancy week tracker, or postpartum recovery.
  • Spiritual: Guided meditations, breathwork, affirmations, and rituals — filtered by your cycle phase and preferences.
  • Community: Connect with others through posts, support circles, and discussions.
  • Planning & Tools: Budget planner, timeline, checklists, appointments, routines, and more.
  • Profile: Settings, preferences, data export, dark mode, and account management.

Managing Your Subscription

Your subscription is managed through the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). To manage or cancel:

  • iOS: Open Settings > tap your name > Subscriptions > Sacred Beginnings.
  • Android: Open Google Play > tap your profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Sacred Beginnings.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You'll retain access to paid features until then.

Restoring Purchases on a New Device

  1. Install Sacred Beginnings on your new device.
  2. Sign in with the same Sacred Beginnings account (same email address).
  3. Go to Profile > Settings > Subscription.
  4. Tap "Restore Purchases". The app will verify your subscription with the App Store or Google Play.
  5. If your subscription is active, all paid features will be restored immediately.

Important: You must use the same Apple ID or Google account that originally purchased the subscription. Cross-platform transfers (iOS to Android or vice versa) are not supported by Apple or Google.

Changing Your Email or Password

  • Email: Go to Profile > Settings > Account > Change Email. You'll need to verify your new email address.
  • Password: Go to Profile > Settings > Account > Change Password. Enter your current password and then your new password. Passwords must meet our security requirements (8+ characters, uppercase, lowercase, number).
  • Forgot password: On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password?" and enter your email. You'll receive a reset link valid for 1 hour.

Deleting Your Account and Data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data:

  1. Go to Profile > Settings > Account > Delete Account.
  2. Read the confirmation warning carefully.
  3. Enter your password to confirm.
  4. All your data (journal entries, cycle data, community posts, health logs, pregnancy data, etc.) will be permanently and irreversibly deleted from our servers.

This action cannot be undone. We recommend exporting your data first (Profile > Settings > Export Data) before deleting your account. If you have an active subscription, remember to cancel it separately through the App Store or Google Play to avoid continued billing.

Troubleshooting

Solutions to common technical issues.

App Is Not Syncing with Apple Health

  1. Open your device's Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Sacred Beginnings and ensure all requested permissions are enabled.
  2. Make sure you have an internet connection — health data syncs to our server when online.
  3. Try pulling down to refresh on the Wellness Dashboard.
  4. Force-close and reopen the app. Health data refreshes on app foreground.
  5. If the issue persists, try toggling the health connection off and on again in Profile > Settings > Health Connection.

Push Notifications Not Working

  1. Check that notifications are enabled for Sacred Beginnings in your device's Settings > Notifications.
  2. In the app, go to Profile > Notification Preferences and ensure the specific notifications you want are toggled on.
  3. Make sure your device is connected to the internet.
  4. On Android, check that Sacred Beginnings is not being restricted by battery optimization. Go to Settings > Battery > Battery Optimization and exclude Sacred Beginnings.
  5. Try logging out and logging back in, which refreshes your push notification token.

Audio Meditations Not Playing

  1. Check that your device is not on silent mode and the volume is turned up.
  2. Ensure you have a stable internet connection — audio streams from our servers.
  3. Close other apps that may be using audio (music players, podcasts, video calls).
  4. If audio cuts out when the screen locks, this is expected behavior on some devices. The app supports background audio playback, but some Android devices may restrict this. Check your device's battery optimization settings.
  5. Try force-closing and reopening the app.
  6. If the issue continues, clear the app's cache (Android: Settings > Apps > Sacred Beginnings > Storage > Clear Cache).

App Crashing or Freezing

  1. Update the app to the latest version from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Restart your device. This resolves many transient issues.
  3. Check available storage. If your device is nearly full, the app may struggle. Free up space if needed.
  4. Force-close and reopen the app.
  5. If crashes persist, try uninstalling and reinstalling the app. Your data is stored on our servers and will be restored when you sign back in.
  6. If the problem continues, please contact us with your device model, OS version, and app version so we can investigate.
