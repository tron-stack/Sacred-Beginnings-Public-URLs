Astrology, Horoscopes & Predictions

Understanding the nature of astrological content in the app

Astrological readings, horoscopes, compatibility scores, numerology results, and all prediction-based content are for entertainment and spiritual reflection only. They should not be taken as factual or used as a basis for major life decisions.

What This Means

  • Daily horoscopes are general readings based on sun sign positions. They are not personalized medical, financial, or relationship advice.
  • Compatibility scores (both Western zodiac and Vedic Ashtakoot) reflect traditional astrological frameworks. They do not predict the success or failure of any relationship.
  • Fertility horoscopes and auspicious day calendars draw from astrological traditions and should not replace medical fertility guidance from your healthcare provider.
  • Numerology analysis (baby name energy, life path numbers) is based on traditional numerological systems and is presented as a fun, reflective exercise.
  • Vedic astrology content (nakshatras, tithis, panchang, dashas) follows traditional Jyotish principles and is presented for cultural and spiritual context.
  • Natal chart interpretations and planetary transits are calculated from astronomical positions but their interpretive meaning is rooted in astrological tradition, not empirical science.
  • Chinese zodiac readings are based on traditional Chinese astrological systems.

Accuracy of Astrological Calculations

Astrological calculations (planetary positions, chart data, nakshatra determination) are computed using astronomical algorithms and third-party services. While the underlying astronomical math is precise, the interpretive layer (what these positions "mean") is a matter of belief and tradition, not scientific fact.

For more accurate birth chart calculations, providing your exact birth time and birth location is recommended. Without these, the app uses default values which may affect the precision of chart-specific readings.

Fertility & Health Information

Important limitations of health-related features

Cycle Tracking & Predictions

The app's cycle prediction engine uses your logged data (period dates, basal body temperature, cervical mucus observations, OPK results) to estimate future cycles, fertile windows, and ovulation timing. These predictions are estimates based on patterns and may not be accurate for every individual or cycle.

Cycle predictions should not be used as a sole method of contraception or as a substitute for professional fertility assessment. If you are having difficulty conceiving, please consult a reproductive endocrinologist or fertility specialist.

Wellness Scores

Wellness scores, alignment scores, fertility readiness scores, and emotional wellness scores are calculated from self-reported data and general wellness principles. They are motivational tools, not clinical assessments. A low or high score does not constitute a medical diagnosis.

Supplement & Nutrition Information

Supplement suggestions and nutrition information in the app are general wellness information. They are not prescriptions. Always consult your healthcare provider before starting any new supplement, especially during preconception, pregnancy, or postpartum periods.
