1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a securely hashed password. We may also collect your selected journey type, spiritual preferences, and partner status during onboarding.

Health & Fertility Data

To provide cycle tracking and fertility insights, we collect data you voluntarily enter, including:

  • Menstrual cycle dates and flow intensity
  • Basal body temperature (BBT) readings
  • Ovulation prediction kit (OPK) results
  • Cervical mucus observations
  • Symptoms (up to 15 types) and moods (up to 10 types)
  • Intimacy logs

Apple HealthKit & Android Health Connect Data

With your explicit permission, we read the following data from Apple HealthKit or Android Health Connect:

  • Sleep duration and quality
  • Step count
  • Heart rate and heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Weight
  • Basal body temperature
  • Hydration / water intake
  • Menstruation data

HealthKit / Health Connect data is read-only. We access this data solely to display health insights within the App. This data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising purposes.

Wellness & Lifestyle Data

We collect wellness data you log within the App, including hydration intake, sleep duration, exercise activity, weight entries, and nutrition logs.

Journal & Personal Entries

We store the content you create within the App, including:

  • Journal entries (daily reflections, gratitude logs)
  • Dream journal entries and symbol interpretations
  • Ancestral healing journal chapters
  • Conception story keepsake chapters
  • Letters to your future self or future child
  • Manifestation intentions and check-ins
  • Body mapping observations and emotional weather reports

Pregnancy & Postpartum Data

If you transition to pregnancy or postpartum mode, we collect pregnancy logs (weight, blood pressure, symptoms, mood, baby movement), due dates, and postpartum recovery data including baby feeding, diaper, and sleep logs, as well as EPDS screening responses.

Community Data

Posts, comments, and interactions you make in community features are stored on our servers. You may choose to post anonymously, in which case your display name is not attached to the content.

Images

Avatar photos and community post images you upload are stored using our image hosting provider (Cloudinary). We do not perform facial recognition or analysis on uploaded images.

AI Chatbot Conversations

When you use the in-app AI assistant, your messages and the assistant's responses are stored in your account. Your messages are sent to Anthropic's API to generate responses (see Section 4).

Partner Data

If you use the Partner Portal, we collect partner invite codes, partner check-in responses, and direct messages between you and your linked partner.

Device Information

We collect your device platform (iOS or Android) solely for the purpose of delivering push notifications. We store push notification tokens provided by Expo Push Notification services.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Location data: We do not collect or track your geographic location.
  • Analytics or tracking data: We do not use analytics SDKs such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or similar tracking tools.
  • Advertising identifiers: We do not collect advertising IDs or participate in ad networks.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and personalize the App's features, including cycle predictions, fertility insights, and phase-aware content recommendations
  • Power the intelligence engine that delivers cross-feature insights and daily alignment scores
  • Deliver push notifications for reminders (practices, journal prompts, supplements, period predictions, moon phases)
  • Send email notifications you have opted into (weekly summaries, fertile window alerts, pregnancy updates)
  • Enable community features and partner communication
  • Process subscription payments through RevenueCat, Apple StoreKit, and Google Play Billing
  • Respond to support requests
  • Improve the App's functionality and fix bugs

We do not use your data for advertising. We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

3. Health Data & Apple HealthKit / Health Connect

We take the handling of health data very seriously. The following commitments apply specifically to data obtained from Apple HealthKit and Android Health Connect:

  • Health data is accessed only with your explicit, opt-in permission.
  • Health data is used solely to display health insights and wellness metrics within the App.
  • Health data is never shared with third parties.
  • Health data is never used for advertising or marketing purposes.
  • Health data is never sold or disclosed to data brokers.
  • You can revoke health data access at any time through your device's system settings.
  • We may write water intake and food log data back to HealthKit / Health Connect with your permission, but only data you explicitly log within the App.

All health data, whether entered manually or synced from HealthKit / Health Connect, is encrypted in transit via HTTPS (TLS 1.2+) and encrypted at rest in our database.

4. AI Chatbot & Anthropic

The Sacred Beginnings AI assistant is powered by Claude, developed by Anthropic. When you use the chatbot:

  • Your messages are sent to Anthropic's API to generate responses.
  • A personalized system prompt containing your cycle phase, journey type, and relevant wellness context is included to provide helpful, contextual responses.
  • Anthropic does not store your conversations for model training purposes.
  • Chat history (up to 50 messages) is stored in your account on our servers so you can review past conversations.
  • The AI assistant is not a medical professional. All chatbot responses include a reminder that the information provided is not medical advice.

For more information about Anthropic's data practices, please review Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Storage & Security

We implement the following security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or higher).
  • Encryption at rest: Your data is stored in MongoDB Atlas with encryption at rest enabled.
  • Password security: Passwords are hashed using industry-standard bcrypt hashing. We never store plaintext passwords.
  • Authentication: We use JWT-based authentication with separate access and refresh tokens. Tokens are stored securely using the device's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, encrypted SharedPreferences on Android).
  • Access control: Administrative access to our systems is restricted and role-based.

While we take reasonable precautions, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Third-party service providers: As described in Section 5, solely to operate the App's features.
  • Partner features: If you link a partner, certain cycle and wellness information is shared with your linked partner as part of the Partner Portal features. You control this by choosing to link or unlink a partner.
  • Community content: Posts and comments you make in community features are visible to other users (anonymously if you choose).
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Safety: We may share information if we believe in good faith that it is necessary to protect the safety of any person or to address fraud or security issues.

  • Partner unlinking: You can disconnect from a linked partner at any time, which revokes their access to your shared data.
