# Sacred Beginnings

**Your sacred journey to motherhood — body, mind, and spirit.**

Sacred Beginnings is a holistic fertility and wellness app for women on the path to motherhood. Whether you're trying to conceive, healing first, currently pregnant, or supporting a partner — Sacred Beginnings meets you where you are.

---

## What Makes Sacred Beginnings Different?

Most fertility apps stop at tracking. Sacred Beginnings goes deeper — combining smart cycle science with guided spiritual practices, journaling, community support, and AI-powered guidance into one beautiful, intuitive experience.

This isn't just a fertility tracker. It's a companion for the most meaningful journey of your life.

## Features

### Fertility & Cycle Tracking
- Adaptive cycle predictions that learn your unique patterns
- Basal body temperature, cervical mucus, and OPK logging
- Fertile window and ovulation alerts
- 15+ symptom and 10+ mood trackers
- Syncs with Apple Health and Android Health Connect

### Guided Spiritual Practices
- 30 meditations, breathwork sessions, affirmations, visualizations, and rituals
- Tailored to your cycle phase (menstrual, follicular, ovulation, luteal)
- Guided audio with ambient sound layering
- Phase-aware recommendations (morning, evening, anytime)

### Journaling & Reflection
- 24 phase-aware guided prompts
- Mood tracking, tags, favorites, and word count stats
- Voice journaling for hands-free reflection
- Dream journal with fertility symbol interpretation
- Ancestral healing journal and conception story keepsake

### AI-Powered Companion
- Personalized chatbot that knows your cycle, mood, and journey stage
- Answers fertility and wellness questions
- Suggests relevant app features and practices
- Always includes appropriate medical disclaimers

### Holistic Wellness
- Hydration, sleep, exercise, weight, and nutrition tracking
- Supplement and medication reminders
- 60+ fertility-friendly recipes organized by cycle phase
- Health scores: Wellness, Calm/Stress, Fertility Readiness
- Apple Health and Health Connect auto-sync

### Conscious Conception Journey
- A signature 6-week guided program: Release, Align, Receive
- 42 days of meditations, journaling, affirmations, and rituals
- Designed to prepare body, mind, and spirit for new life

### Moon Phases & Astrology
- All 8 moon phases with fertility implications and energy patterns
- Daily horoscope and fertility horoscope (cycle phase x zodiac)
- Vedic astrology with 27 Nakshatras and auspicious days
- Cycle-moon sync detection
- New/Full moon push notifications

### Partner Portal
- Invite your partner with a shareable code
- Shared cycle view (phase, fertile window, predictions)
- Daily communication prompts and check-ins
- Private messaging
- Intimacy calendar (physical, emotional, spiritual, communication)

### Community & Support
- Posts across 8 categories (TTC, Spiritual Motherhood, IVF Support, and more)
- Anonymous posting and commenting
- Support circles with invite codes
- Success stories wall
- Moderated for safety

### Pregnancy & Postpartum
- Week-by-week pregnancy content for all 42 weeks
- Baby size comparisons, development details, and weekly tips
- Postpartum recovery timeline (12 weeks)
- EPDS depression screening with crisis resources
- Breastfeeding support and baby tracking

### Planning Tools
- Budget planner with savings goals
- Timeline planner with auto-generated milestones
- 6 checklists: Preconception, First Trimester, Hospital Bag, Nursery Prep, Postpartum Essentials, Financial Planning
- Appointment tracker with 10 provider types
- Customizable daily routines

### 40+ Educational Resources
Expert articles covering fertility basics, nutrition, holistic health, emotional wellness, medical info, partner support, and success stories.

### More Features
- Womb Wisdom Oracle (40 archetype cards)
- Body mapping (11 tappable zones, sensation tracking)
- Manifestation tracker with weekly check-ins
- Sound healing library (15 curated frequencies and nature sounds)
- Baby name energy matcher (numerology and element association)
- Emotional weather reports with pattern analysis
- Monthly ritual planner (cycle x moon x zodiac)
- Cycle-phase beauty and skincare tips
- Daily alignment score with streak tracking
- Weekly challenges
- Sacred Beginnings Wrapped (annual summary)
- Shareable bump cards and milestone stories
- Data export (journal, cycles, pregnancy, all)
- Dark mode with warm espresso tones

## Medical Disclaimer

Sacred Beginnings is a wellness and lifestyle companion. It is **not a medical device** and does **not provide medical advice**. Cycle predictions and fertility insights are informational only. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical decisions, fertility treatment, or mental health concerns.

The EPDS screening feature is informational and is **not a diagnosis**. If you or someone you know is in crisis:
- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline**: Call or text 988
- **Postpartum Support International**: 1-800-944-4773
- **Crisis Text Line**: Text HOME to 741741
